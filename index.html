Loci Kids Learning — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Loci Kids Learning” (package app.loci.kids)

The short version: Loci collects no personal data. Everything a child does in the app stays on the device it was done on. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that could send data anywhere.

Who Loci is for

Loci is a learning app for children aged roughly 6–8, set up and supervised by a parent or carer (“grown-up”). It is designed to meet Google Play’s Families policy and the principles of COPPA and GDPR-K: no data is collected from children, and no data is shared with anyone.

What is stored — and where

InformationWhere it livesWhy
Child profile: first name or nickname, avatar emoji, age bandOn the device only (app database)To greet the child and choose age-appropriate lessons
Learning progress: lessons completed, answers, stars, streak days, skill ratingsOn the device onlyTo plan the next lesson and show progress to the child and grown-up
Grown-up settings: PIN (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), reward ladder, screen-time and quiet-hours rules, reminder timeOn the device onlyTo protect the grown-ups area and apply the family’s rules

None of this is transmitted, backed up to our servers (we have none) or included in Android cloud backup — backup is disabled for the app. Deleting a child profile in the grown-ups area erases every record about them; uninstalling the app erases everything.

Device permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only if a grown-up turns on the daily practice reminder. The reminder is generated on the device and contains no personal information.
  • Microphone (optional, only if a grown-up turns it on): lets a child say a word back to Lumi in the listening activities. Speech is recognised on the device using the phone’s built-in offline recogniser; audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. If the device cannot recognise speech offline, the feature is simply unavailable and the tap-the-sounds version is used instead.
  • Loci does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, storage or any identifier.

Text-to-speech

Lumi’s voice uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. Loci only passes it the words to read aloud; it receives nothing back.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

There are none. Loci is free, contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app in the child’s area, and no analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Children’s privacy

Because no data leaves the device, we never receive personal information from children or anyone else. A grown-up can review or delete everything the app holds from within the grown-ups area at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever needs to store or send data differently, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described in the app’s update notes. The effective date above always reflects the current version.
